## Title: AI for Kids (Grades 6–10) — Episode 1: What is AI?

### Audience
- Students in classes 6–10 (approx. ages 11–16)

### Duration
- ~6 minutes

### Learning Objectives (by the end, students can…)
- Explain in simple words what Artificial Intelligence (AI) is.
- Distinguish AI vs Machine Learning (ML) vs Deep Learning (DL) at a high level.
- Describe how machines learn from data using examples.
- Identify at least three real-life uses of AI they see daily.
- Recognize basic ethics ideas: bias, privacy, and responsible use.
- Follow instructions for a short hands-on “paper AI” activity.

### Key Vocabulary
- Data: Information we use to learn patterns.
- Model: A computer’s learned “recipe” that makes predictions.
- Algorithm: The step-by-step method the computer follows.

### Episode Outline (6:00)
1) Hook: “Have you already used AI today?” (0:00–0:25)
2) What is AI? Simple definition + examples (0:25–1:10)
3) AI vs ML vs DL — the nested boxes idea (1:10–2:00)
4) How machines learn: data → patterns → predictions (2:00–3:00)
5) Real-life examples (assistants, recommendations, translation) (3:00–3:40)
6) Ethics: bias, privacy, safety (3:40–4:20)
7) Mini activity: “Paper Classifier” with simple rules (4:20–5:30)
8) Series preview + recap + quiz teaser (5:30–6:00)

### Teaching Approach
- Use everyday analogies (teacher grading, sorting cards, playlists).
- Keep visuals big and clear; minimal text on slides.
- Invite learners to pause and try the mini activity.

### Materials Needed (for activity)
- Paper, pen/pencil, a few sticky notes or index cards.
