# Quiz (5 Questions)

1) What does AI help computers do?
- A) Store files only
- B) Do tasks that need human-like smarts
- C) Make computers heavier
- D) Turn off the internet
Answer: B

2) Which is inside which?
- A) DL ⟶ ML ⟶ AI
- B) AI ⟶ ML ⟶ DL
- C) ML ⟶ AI ⟶ DL
- D) AI ⟶ DL ⟶ ML
Answer: B

3) What do machines learn from in ML?
- A) Guessing only
- B) Magic
- C) Data and examples
- D) Random numbers
Answer: C

4) Which is an example of AI?
- A) A pencil
- B) A calendar drawn on paper
- C) Face unlock on a phone
- D) A cardboard box
Answer: C

5) What is bias in AI?
- A) A type of battery
- B) When the model is unfair because the data is unfair
- C) When the computer sleeps
- D) When the screen is bright
Answer: B

# Hands-on Activity: Paper Classifier

Materials: 5 sticky notes/index cards, pen/pencil.
Steps:
1) Draw five doodles: sun, cloud, tree, house, smiley.
2) Make a rule: “If it has eyes or a mouth → Face; else → Not Face.”
3) Sort your doodles into two groups.
4) Improve your rules with features: shape, roof, eyes.
5) Try predicting each doodle using your rules; check and adjust.
